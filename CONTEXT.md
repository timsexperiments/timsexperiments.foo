# Tim's Experiments

Tim's Experiments presents Tim's software engineering work and writing through an interactive research campus. Its primary audience is prospective freelance and fractional software engineering clients, with prospective employers as another audience.

## Language

**Research campus**:
The futuristic, cinematic setting visitors explore to encounter the site's content and optional interactions. It has no required storyline.
_Avoid_: Story campaign, inventor's workshop

**Article challenge**:
An interactive exercise associated with an article that lets a reader try the concepts discussed in that article.
_Avoid_: Required quest

**Recipe**:
A software engineering article presented as a readable item within the lab. The term describes its presentation in the campus, not a separate kind of content.
_Avoid_: Required quest item

**Minigame**:
An optional playful activity within the campus, which may exist independently of an article challenge.
_Avoid_: Required progression
